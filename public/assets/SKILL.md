## SanLuis at a glance
- **Two colors and a paper:** Atlantic Navy `#1A3160`, Heritage Gold `#D9A434`, Bone `#FAF7F1`. Gold is a *glint*, never a wash.
- **Type pairing:** Cormorant Garamond (display, italics, headlines through h4) + Inter (body, UI, h5/h6) + JetBrains Mono (data, code).
- **Sharp edges:** default radius 4px. No glassmorphism. No bouncy animation. Tonal navy shadows only.
- **Voice:** declarative, expensive, surgical. No emoji. No exclamation marks. Em dashes welcome. Periods on every UI string.
- **Iconography:** Lucide at 1.5px stroke. Brand glyphs (flame, diamond, ornament-divider) live in `assets/`.
- **Imagery:** warm, low-key, architectural. No people-on-laptops stock. No abstract AI swooshes.
